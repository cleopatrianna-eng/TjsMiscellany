The Writer at the Desk

Welcome to Tj's Miscellany — a corner of the internet devoted to the things I find my brian constantly thinking about.

I am not a writer nor an editor. For years I have worked in offices, in data of all things — first as an analyst and now a manager, but I have a lot of interests beyond my work. Books, art, gardening, history, architecture, science, design...my brain is never not thinking and I'd like to think I can give it something better to do than doomscroll and watch K-Dramas.

"I write about the things that interest me. They may not last as an interest, they still be something I go back to in 20 years. Who Knows?"

This is my attempt to learn how to create something of interest (if only to me) and use github and claude (as a generator of code - not art and literature!) — I'll only write and share what I actually want to and probably not very often - if at all. I've a very bad habit of losing interest in things, let's see if this one lasts.

You'll find book reviews here that don't waffle — I will share what I liked and why, not literary critiques - there's plenty of better people out there already doing that (I'll share them as well). You'll find art writing that will shift from great masters i've seen down to awesome local artists I find when out and about. You'll find essays about all manner of gubbins. And in the Emporium, you'll find things I have bought and can confidently say I thought were worth it. These aren't sponsored or even affiliated at the moment so no benefit to me of sharing other than the incurable belief that my opinion is worth something sometimes.

I publish when I have something worth saying, which means irregularly. I have no newsletter nor social media at this point - let's see where we get to but I make no promises.
