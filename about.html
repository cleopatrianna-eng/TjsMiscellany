The Writer at the Desk

Welcome to Tj's Miscellany — a corner of the internet devoted to the things I find most worth thinking about. Literature, largely. Art, consistently. Objects with histories. Ideas that resist being summarised. The kind of things that make you stop mid-sentence and reach for a pencil.

I came to writing the long way round. For years I worked in libraries — first as a cataloguer, then in rare books — and it was there, surrounded by things that had outlasted their owners, that I began to understand what it means for something to matter. Books are not just texts. They are objects with lives. The notes in the margins, the pressed flowers between pages, the names written in fading ink on the flyleaf — all of it tells a story beyond the one that was printed.

"I write about the things that compel me. Not the new, necessarily, but the interesting. Not the fashionable, but the fine."

This magazine started as a newsletter — a few hundred words sent to a handful of friends each month. It has grown into something more structured, though I hope not less personal. Every piece I publish here is written because I wanted to write it, not because an algorithm suggested a gap in the market.

You'll find book reviews here that take their time — I don't believe in star ratings or brevity for its own sake. You'll find art writing that tries to describe what it actually felt like to stand in front of something. You'll find essays about reading, about collecting, about the quiet art of paying attention. And in the Emporium, you'll find things I have bought or coveted or simply admired — editions, stationery, objects that reward a long acquaintance.

I publish when I have something worth saying, which means irregularly but sincerely. The newsletter goes out on the first of each month. If you'd like it to arrive in your inbox, there is a subscription link at the bottom of this page.
