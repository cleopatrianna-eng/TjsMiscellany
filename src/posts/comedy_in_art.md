The most fun I ever had at an art gallery or museum was the Cat Museum in Amsterdam.

Mostly because of one massive wall sized painting that me and my husband stared at for 5 minutes before we realised where the cat was. We were in hysterics, especially when we realised the painting was over 120 years old. 

Not everything has to be serious.
